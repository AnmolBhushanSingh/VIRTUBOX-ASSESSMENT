Digital Boarding is #1: Mobile check-in and boarding ease is the single strongest predictor of satisfaction ($r = 0.495$); flyers rating boarding $\ge 4/5$ have an 82% satisfaction rate (versus 13% for $\le 3$).  Economy Cabin Disparity: Economy satisfaction is in crisis at 19.4% (versus 69.5% in Business Class) and represents 73% of all dissatisfied flyers.  Comfort Trumps Food: Inflight entertainment and seat comfort correlate nearly twice as strongly with satisfaction as catering quality.  Service Buffers Delays: Even during major delays (>60 minutes), 65% of flyers remain satisfied if cabin and ground service are exceptional.  Simpson's Paradox Discovery: Flight time convenience showed an illusory negative correlation in aggregate ($r = -0.065$) because dissatisfied leisure travelers disproportionately select convenient slots while happy business travelers fly inconvenient red-eyes. Upgrade Economy Cabin Essentials (Priority 3): Reallocate catering renegotiation budgets into ergonomic seat cushions, USB-C in-seat charging, and mobile streaming entertainment.
